## Source and attribution

The data is adapted from plot synopses and character appearance lists on the [Marvel Database](https://marvel.fandom.com) (Fandom). Those pages are licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0, which allows adaptations to be shared under a later version of the same licence. Every record links to the wiki page it came from. The page history there lists the contributors to each page.

Changes made: winners, losers, method and confidence were extracted by an AI model (Claude), with a one-sentence summary of how each win happened and a short quote from the synopsis. Errors in the extraction are ours, not the wiki's.

If you reuse the data, credit "comic-win-chains, adapted from the Marvel Database (Fandom)" and share your adaptations under CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible licence.
